American Express Airport Lounge Access & Centurion Lounge Privileges

1. Global Lounge Collection Access:
- Platinum and Centurion cardmembers receive complimentary access to 1,550+ airport lounges across 140 countries.
- Lounges included: The Centurion® Lounge network, Priority Pass™ Select (enrollment required), Delta Sky Club® (when flying Delta), Escape Lounges - The Centurion Studio Partner, Plaza Premium Lounges, and Lufthansa Lounges (when flying Lufthansa Group).

2. Centurion Lounge Terms & Access Rules:
- Access is permitted within 3 hours of scheduled departure time upon presenting a valid Platinum Card, same-day boarding pass, and government-issued ID.
- Guest Policy: Platinum Cardmembers who spend $75,000+ on eligible purchases in a calendar year earn complimentary guest access for up to two guests per visit. Otherwise, guest passes are available for $50 per adult ($30 for children ages 2-17).
- Amenities: Premium complimentary dining curated by award-winning chefs, signature craft cocktails, high-speed Wi-Fi, shower suites, and private work sanctuaries.
