American Express® Gold Card Benefits, Multipliers, and Points Guide

1. 4X Membership Rewards Points:
- Earn 4X Membership Rewards® points at Restaurants worldwide, including takeout and delivery in the US.
- Earn 4X Membership Rewards® points at US Supermarkets on up to $25,000 per calendar year in purchases (then 1X).
- Strategy to maximize: Use the AmEx Gold Card as your primary card for all grocery shopping, dining out, food delivery services (Uber Eats, Grubhub, DoorDash), and meal kit subscriptions.

2. 3X Points on Travel:
- Earn 3X Membership Rewards® points on flights booked directly with airlines or on amextravel.com.

3. Statement Credits & Monthly Benefits:
- $120 Dining Credit: Earn up to $10 in statement credits monthly when you pay with the Gold Card at participating dining partners including Grubhub, Seamless, The Cheesecake Factory, Goldbelly, Milk Bar, and select Shake Shack locations. Enrollment required.
- $120 Uber Cash: Get $10 in Uber Cash each month for Uber Eats orders or Uber rides in the US when you add your Gold Card to your Uber account.
- $100 Resy Credit: Receive up to $100 annually ($50 semi-annually) in statement credits on eligible Resy purchases.
- $84 Dunkin' Credit: Earn up to $7 monthly in statement credits on eligible Dunkin' purchases.

4. How to Increase & Maximize Your AmEx Gold Card Benefits:
- Enroll in AmEx Offers: Log into your AmEx App or online account monthly and add targeted AmEx Offers to your Gold Card to earn extra points (e.g., +5X points per $1 spent or $20 back on $100 spend).
- Stack Multipliers: Combine the 4X dining multiplier with dining rewards programs or Rakuten (set to earn AmEx Membership Rewards points instead of cash back) when ordering food online.
- Transfer Points to Airline & Hotel Partners: Maximize point value by transferring Membership Rewards points to 1:1 transfer partners like Delta, British Airways, Singapore Airlines, Air Canada Aeroplan, and Marriott Bonvoy during bonus transfer promotions (e.g., 20%–30% bonus transfers).
